WHAT'S HAPPENING

A few image-processing steps.
No trained model.

The demo subtracts a running background, keeps the bright pixels, and combines the two masks. Erosion removes small specks. The largest remaining contour becomes the target.

The example is a generated scene passed through the same OpenCV pipeline as the camera.
